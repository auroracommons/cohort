Learn

How Cohort works, from first click to first company.

1

Pick a handle

No signup, no password — choose a handle and you're in. It identifies you in workspaces, chats and upvotes.

2

Learn by contributing to open source

Open the Open Source tab: every project listed has tasks marked as good for a first try. Pick one that fits your interests and read its guide for new helpers. Then make your own copy of the project, work on the fix in a separate branch, and send the change back as a pull request. One finished pull request teaches you more than ten guides. It also shows others what you can do.

3

Join or start a project

Browse Projects to find teams looking for your skills, or start your own with a one-breath pitch and “looking for” tags. Link your GitHub repo so contributors can find the code.

4

Build together in the workspace

Every project has a shared workspace that updates as you type: a page everyone can write on and a team chat. Write plans together and plan the release. Everyone sees each change at once.

5

Bring your own AI

Open AI Settings, pick a provider and paste an API key. Gemini, Groq and OpenRouter all have free tiers (no credit card). Then type @ai in any team chat: the whole team sees the question and the answer. Your key stays in your browser.

6

Grow it into a business

Make something small, show it to ten real users, and change it based on what they say. More upvotes move you up the Leaderboard, and more people see your project and may join it. The News tab shows what founders and hackers are talking about now.

Start a project

Pitch it in one breath. You can refine everything later in the workspace.

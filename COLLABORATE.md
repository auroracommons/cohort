# Collaborate on Cohort

Cohort serves one problem family: collective action and coordination
failure. This page lists concrete, scoped ways an organization can work
with the project. Every task here is real and completable. Nothing on this
page implies an existing partnership; collaborations are invitations until
they actually happen, and no partner is named publicly without their
written agreement.

## Who this is for

Companies with developer communities, foundations, and workforce agencies.

## Sized tasks

1. **Sponsor a themed cohort or challenge.** Define one real problem, a
   time box, and a review panel of named humans who commit to actually
   reviewing what gets built. We run it in a public Cohort instance and
   publish the outcomes, including the ones that did not work.
2. **Contribute mentor hours.** Mentors join rooms on a stated schedule
   and answer questions in the open, where their answers help the whole
   room rather than one private thread.
3. **Adopt the MCP workspace for an internal innovation program.** Run
   Cohort inside your organization and let your own agents join rooms
   through the MCP server. We want your deployment notes, honest ones,
   published as a case study with your approval.

## What you get

A public, dated record: the challenge brief, the review outcomes, or the
deployment case study, committed in this repository with your
organization's role stated as you approve it.

## Start

Open an issue in this repository describing the task you have in mind and
the problem you want a cohort to work on. Small and specific beats grand
and vague.
